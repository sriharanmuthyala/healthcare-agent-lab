# Healthcare Agent Lab: complete opportunity map

Prepared by Natasha · 9 October 2026

All 115 entries in the supplied Google healthcare extract, all 52 entries under Healthcare headings in the Microsoft page, and one supplementary MEDITECH excerpt. Retains the original 18 workflows. Cross-sector healthcare examples outside these sections are not an exhaustive scan.

Company examples remain separate. Multiple examples can map to one workflow, and one example can map to multiple workflows. Repeated company names are not counted as unique organizations.

The initial five are provider inbox, document intake and matching, protocol and policy search, call quality review, and appointment coordination. Scores describe fictional-data prototype suitability and are analyst judgments. The present inbox demonstration uses rules. New workflows are mapped proposals, not completed AI prototypes.

## Sources

- [Deloitte Life Sciences & Health Care AI Dossier](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Z1I6e98nShIRpK49fpKeXwX0JOm5WIcf/view): Ideas and potential benefits, not independent validation. US payer workflows need local adaptation.
- [Stack AI: 25 Use Cases Transforming Industries](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1bL54_-FG9_S10AzcLO8AlInfI0jJ-i-i/view): Benefits are vendor claims. Some data-source labels appear inconsistent, including the SOAP note example.
- [HHS Artificial Intelligence Use Cases FY2022](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1j4zuAbm2o5_zz4GEG20eLPLY50iwztf8/view): Historical examples. NIH grant routing is not clinical patient referral routing.
- [NHS AI expansion for missed appointments](https://www.england.nhs.uk/2024/03/nhs-ai-expansion-to-help-tackle-missed-appointments-and-improve-waiting-times/): Attendance prediction evidence supports the opportunity. Our initial demo only uses deterministic scheduling.
- [ADHA Service Referral Information Requirements](https://implementer.digitalhealth.gov.au/resources/service-referral-information-requirements-v1-1): Content reference, not evidence that an AI referral assistant improves outcomes.
- [Australian Commission AI Clinical Use Guide](https://www.safetyandquality.gov.au/resources/ai-clinical-use-guide): Clinical outputs require accountable review.
- [Google Cloud industry examples: healthcare and life sciences](https://cloud.google.com/transform/101-real-world-generative-ai-use-cases-from-industry-leaders): Customer stories and supplier-reported outcomes. Mapping describes proposed adaptations, not replicated benefits.
- [Microsoft 1,000+ customer stories: Healthcare sections](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-cloud/blog/2025/07/24/ai-powered-success-with-1000-stories-of-customer-transformation-and-innovation/): Healthcare headings reviewed on 9 October 2026. Some stories describe conventional AI or cloud infrastructure rather than generative agents.
- [User-supplied Google Workspace healthcare examples](https://cloud.google.com/transform/101-real-world-generative-ai-use-cases-from-industry-leaders): Supplemental MEDITECH example supplied by user. Exact underlying customer story has not been independently verified.
