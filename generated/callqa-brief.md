# Call quality review assistant

User: Quality reviewer

Problem: Manual quality checks are slow and inconsistent.

Workflow: Assess a fictional call transcript against a checklist and cite evidence.

Input: 10 fictional call transcripts and a service-quality checklist.

Output: Evidence-linked checklist findings and review queue.

Measure: Checklist agreement and unsupported findings

Review: Human approval before live action. Clinical outputs require a qualified reviewer.

Source: Stack AI: 25 Use Cases Transforming Industries, p.27 Call Center QA Agent
https://drive.google.com/file/d/1bL54_-FG9_S10AzcLO8AlInfI0jJ-i-i/view

Adaptation: Direct workflow, text input for first prototype.

Score: 93/100. Initial analyst judgement, not validated ROI.
