# Provider inbox assistant

User: Practice coordinator

Problem: Mixed clinical and administrative messages slow review.

Workflow: Classify messages, retrieve approved administrative answers and draft replies.

Input: 20 fictional booking, billing and document enquiries.

Output: Category, reviewer queue and grounded draft reply.

Measure: Routing accuracy and review time

Review: Human approval before live action. Clinical outputs require a qualified reviewer.

Source: Deloitte Life Sciences & Health Care AI Dossier, pp.23–24 Provider in-basket management
https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Z1I6e98nShIRpK49fpKeXwX0JOm5WIcf/view

Adaptation: Direct workflow, narrowed to administrative drafting.

Score: 97/100. Initial analyst judgement, not validated ROI.
