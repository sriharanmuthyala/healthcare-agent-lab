# Appointment coordination assistant

User: Clinic coordinator

Problem: Cancellations create unused capacity and coordination work.

Workflow: Propose compatible replacement slots and waitlist candidates for approval.

Input: A fictional calendar with 50 slots and 12 waitlist entries.

Output: Eligible slots, constraints and draft messages.

Measure: Valid proposals and conflict prevention

Review: Human approval before live action. Clinical outputs require a qualified reviewer.

Source: NHS AI expansion for missed appointments, March 2024 missed-appointments programme
